# Motun's Steps and Outfit — first website stage

## How to preview
1. Download and extract this ZIP.
2. Open the `motuns-steps-and-outfit` folder.
3. Double-click `index.html` to preview in your browser.

## Included
- Responsive storefront layout for phones and computers
- Pink, cream and black brand styling
- Eight product categories
- Sample product cards with search and category filtering
- Working add-to-bag, quantity controls, remove item and subtotal
- Reserved customer account/login area
- WhatsApp contact link

## Important
This is the first visual/storefront stage, not a complete live ecommerce system.
- Product names, prices and photos are samples. Replace them with the real catalogue.
- Login/registration is a placeholder; no real authentication is connected yet.
- Checkout and payments are not connected to Paystack or any other provider.
- There is no database, owner admin dashboard, inventory system, delivery SMS, or automatic payment confirmation yet.
- Confirm the WhatsApp contact number and all store information before publishing.
- Online images and fonts require internet access.

We will add the remaining systems step by step after the storefront design is approved.
